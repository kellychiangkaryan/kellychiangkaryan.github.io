Kelly’s Crystal

An online shop for crystals, built for an advanced web development module. Shoppers can browse the catalogue, search, read and leave reviews, and check out, while admins manage products, orders and users.

Role
Developer & designer
Team
Solo
Duration
2022
Tech
ASP.NET Web Forms, C#, SQL Server, HTML/CSS

From a bookstore to a crystal shop

The module gave us a starter template for an online bookstore. I turned it into my own shop: I rewrote the CSS and reworked the page layouts, replaced the books with a catalogue of 25 crystals across five categories, and wrote the content pages, including an About page and a guide to cleansing crystals.

What the site does

  • Accounts: shoppers can create an account and sign in, with passwords stored as salted hashes. Admins have a separate sign-in.
  • Browsing: a catalogue sorted into bracelets, tumbles, towers, spheres and cubes, each showing where the crystal comes from, plus a search bar.
  • Product pages: price, add to cart and wishlist, and customer reviews with star ratings.
  • Checkout: a shopping cart, then shipping and payment pages.
  • Admin pages: manage users and orders, and add new products with an image upload.
  • Database: products, users, orders and reviews are stored in SQL Server.
