About me

Designer who codes, and a tester who designs.

I'm studying Computer Science in Interactive Media and Game Development at SIT-DigiPen. I'm most interested in how a space, a light or a sound can tell players where to go and how to feel, without a single UI prompt.

Before DigiPen I worked at Accenture as a Techno-Functional Business Analyst. I led User Acceptance Testing with clients, turned feedback into clear fixes, and learned to explain complex systems to non-technical people. I bring that same loop of test, listen, iterate to every game I work on.

I mainly play multiplayer games like Overwatch and Dead by Daylight, and I've recently fallen for story-driven indie games like OneShot, Undertale and Corpse Party. Most of my design ideas come from horror games and how they build tension and atmosphere. That's what drew me to The Unraveling, where I built spatial sound so players can hear where things are around them and feel immersed in the school.

I'm looking for a design internship or a business and technology internship. I enjoy roles where I get to work with lots of different people.
