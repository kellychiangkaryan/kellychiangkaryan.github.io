Audio: spatial sound with FMOD

I built a dynamic spatial audio system in C++ and C# on top of FMOD. It uses Digital Signal Processing (DSP) crossfading and custom low-pass filters to make the world feel more immersive.

Try the concept

This small demo uses synthesised sound in your browser to show the two techniques. It is a simplified illustration, not the game's actual audio. Use headphones and keep the volume low.

Low-pass filter
