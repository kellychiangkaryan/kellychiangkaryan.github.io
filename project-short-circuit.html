Short Circuit

Short Circuit is a puzzle battle game. Players travel across a map of battles, shops and treasure, and fight enemies by placing circuit pieces on a grid to build a closed loop. Elemental pieces deal extra damage to enemies weak to that element.

Roles
Project Manager, Gameplay & UI Programmer, Icon Design
Team
Team of 5
Duration
2024
Tech
C, CProcessing

Trailer

What I did

  • Enemies: I set up the three enemy types (Wizard, Plant and Vulture), each with its own health, attack, weakness and resistance. I also coded the random spawning, which picks the enemy types and places them on random, non-overlapping cells of the grid.
  • Battle won and lost screens: the end screens, with a button that resets the run and returns to the main menu.
  • Hover info in battle: hovering over an enemy or boss part shows its health, weakness and resistance, and hovering over a circuit piece shows its element.
  • Map hover and paths: hovering over a node on the map describes the event ahead, and the paths from the player's current node are highlighted.
  • Element logos: I designed the logos for each element.
  • Project management: I was the project manager for the group.
