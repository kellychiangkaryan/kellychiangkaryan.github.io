Smart Study Room

An IoT system for booking and monitoring study rooms at Nanyang Polytechnic. A Raspberry Pi with sensors tracks each room’s noise, temperature and lighting, and students tap their student card to check in. I built the room booking side of the student app.

Role
Developer: booking form and booking history
Team
Team of 4
Duration
2021 – 2022
Tech
C#, Windows Forms, SQL Server, Raspberry Pi

The idea

Our brief was to build a smart solution with at least one user input sensor and one environment sensor. We chose study rooms. Students book a room and check in with their student card on an RFID reader, a light shows whether the room is taken, and they can turn a knob to adjust the light. A sound sensor and a temperature sensor let staff spot rooms that are too noisy or too hot or cold. Students who don’t show up for their booking can be blacklisted.

My part: booking

I built the booking form and booking history in the student app, along with the booking table they save to.

Booking form

  • Student details filled in: the form picks up the logged-in student, so they only enter who’s attending, the date, time slot, group size and room.
  • Date rules: students can book up to 14 days ahead, but not in the past or on weekends.
  • Checks before saving: the booking is saved only when every field is filled in, with a clear message if something is missing.

Booking history

  • Own bookings only: each student sees just their own bookings, newest first.
  • Cancelling: students can cancel an upcoming booking after confirming. Past bookings can only be viewed.
